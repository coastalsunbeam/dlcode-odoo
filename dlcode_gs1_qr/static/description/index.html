DLCode GS1 QR Codes

Turn the barcodes you already have into GS1 Digital Link QR codes.

One QR code that works at the till and on the shopper's phone. DLCode reads the GTIN in your product barcode, registers it, and gives you a print-ready GS1 QR label. Shoppers who scan it land on your product page.

How it works

  1. Create an API key at dlcode.org.
  2. Paste it in Settings, DLCode.
  3. Select products and choose Action, Sync to DLCode. Or click Sync to DLCode on the DLCode tab of a product.
  4. The DLCode tab shows the code link and the QR label. Turn on auto-sync to send a product each time its barcode changes.

Only products with a valid GTIN barcode (8, 12, 13 or 14 digits) are sent. If eCommerce is installed, published products point to their shop page.

Works with Odoo 17.0 and 18.0.
