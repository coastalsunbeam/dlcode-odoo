DLCode Lot Recalls

Mark a lot recalled in Odoo. Shoppers who scan that lot see your notice.

Your GS1 QR codes from DLCode carry the lot code. This module adds a Recalled checkbox and a note for shoppers to each lot in Odoo. Save the lot, and from the next scan, people with that lot see your recall notice. Other lots keep opening the normal page.

How it works

  1. Install DLCode GS1 QR Codes and add your DLCode API key in Settings, DLCode.
  2. Turn on Lots & Serial Numbers in Inventory, Configuration, Settings.
  3. Open a lot in Inventory, Products, Lots/Serial Numbers.
  4. In the DLCode recall section, tick Recalled and write what shoppers should do. Save.
  5. Untick Recalled and save to end the recall for that lot.

If DLCode cannot be reached or says no, Odoo shows the error and does not save, so Odoo and DLCode always match.

Recalls need a paid DLCode pack. Lot codes follow GS1 rules: up to 20 letters, digits or - . / _ with no spaces. A recall notice adds to, and does not replace, your official recall notification.

Setup guide: dlcode.org/guides/sync-recalls-from-odoo. Works with Odoo 17.0 and 18.0.
